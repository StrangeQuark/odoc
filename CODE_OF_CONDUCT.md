# Odoc Code of Conduct

**Status:** proposed public-release policy; operational private reporting/appeal contacts
are required before the first public release.

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in Odoc welcoming and safe for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or
technology choice.

We will act and interact in ways that support an open, respectful, collaborative,
and technically rigorous community. Criticism of ideas is welcome; degradation of
people is not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Assume good intent initially while taking reported impact seriously.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; explain tradeoffs and cite evidence.
- Respect boundaries, pronouns, privacy, attribution, and requests to stop.
- Make space for less-heard participants and help others learn without gatekeeping.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and recuse when appropriate.
- Protect credentials, private documentation, vulnerability details, and personal data.
- Accept moderation decisions while using the documented appeal process.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or sustained unwanted contact.
- Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, or deliberate outing.
- Insults, demeaning comments, discriminatory jokes, trolling, or personal attacks.
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information without permission.
- Impersonation, deceptive attribution, spam, or manipulation of community processes.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.
- Knowingly exposing credentials, exploits, private content, or personal data in public.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This policy applies in Odoc repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, chat,
events, and other official spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents
Odoc, and to off-project conduct that creates a credible safety risk for participants.

## Reporting

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Before this policy is adopted or
outside contributions are solicited, maintainers must publish a monitored private
conduct contact and a different independent appeal contact, document who can access
each, and test both routes. Those contacts are currently pending; this policy is not
operational before a public release. For an immediate
GitHub-hosted safety issue in the meantime, use GitHub's own block/report-abuse controls,
but understand that this does not deliver a report to the Odoc project.

If a report involves a maintainer, that person is recused. If it involves the project
owner, an independent unconflicted moderator must handle it. Reports will be shared only
with people needed to assess safety and enforce this policy. Confidentiality cannot be
absolute when disclosure is needed to prevent harm or required by law, but reporters
will be told when safely possible.

Include only information you are comfortable sharing: what happened, where and when,
relevant context/evidence, immediate safety needs, and desired outcome. Anonymous and
third-party reports will be considered, though limited information can constrain action.

## Enforcement

Maintainers consider context, impact, pattern, power imbalance, safety, and the wishes
of affected people. Possible responses include a private clarification, warning,
content removal, interaction limits, temporary suspension, permanent ban, role/access
removal, or referral to platform or event safety teams.

Except where immediate safety requires action, the subject receives notice and a fair
opportunity to respond. Enforcement records are access-limited and retained only as
long as needed for safety, consistency, and appeal. Public statements minimize personal
information and do not expose reporters without consent.

An affected person or subject may request review by an unconflicted maintainer within
30 days. The appeal reviews process, proportionality, and material new evidence; it is
not a public re-litigation. The project owner is the final appeal during the founder-led
stage unless involved, in which case an independent moderator is final.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers must apply this policy consistently and proportionately. A maintainer who
ignores, conceals, retaliates over, or abuses an enforcement process may lose project
authority under [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

This policy should be reviewed when the community adds new communication channels or
events and at least once per major release.
