# Odoc working-name and official-channel policy

**Status:** proposed foundation decision; project-owner approval is required before
`P0-001` is complete.

The name **Odoc is a temporary repository locator only and is not recommended as the
public product name**. The project's
[collision and replacement-name screen](docs/project/name-collision-research.md) found an exact,
active OCaml developer-documentation tool plus other package/software/name conflicts.
A replacement candidate must be selected, expanded screening completed, and adoption
separately approved before `P0-002` fixes package/artifact/runtime names.
The project has not completed legal name/trademark clearance, identified a trademark
rights holder, adopted an official logo, or represented the name as registered. This
file is a project-identity guideline, not a claim of exclusive trademark rights or a
purported license of rights the project may not own.

The open-source license grants copyright permissions in covered software and
documentation. It does not make a third-party fork, service, artifact, account, event,
or statement official or endorsed by the Odoc project.

## Factual and lawful use

Nothing here restricts fair use, nominative use, criticism, commentary, comparative
advertising, legally required notices, or any other right granted by applicable law.
Examples of project uses that are encouraged when accurate include:

- truthfully referring or linking to the Odoc repositories;
- saying software is compatible with, based on, or forked from a named Odoc version;
- reporting, teaching, researching, reviewing, criticizing, or discussing the project;
- retaining copyright, license, provenance, and attribution notices in a distribution.

Compatibility and Confluence-parity statements must name the tested version and scope.
They must not imply certification, security review, support, sponsorship, or endorsement
that did not occur.

## What the project calls official

During the founder-led stage, the project owner identified in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) administers the official repositories, releases,
package coordinates, domains, accounts, and any future visual assets. That is an
administrative designation for project channels, not evidence of trademark ownership.

The project will not designate the following as official without recorded approval:

- a fork, hosted service, commercial product, event, certification, or merchandise;
- a domain, package, application, social account, container, chart, or release mirror;
- a modified logo or a name/identity likely to confuse users about release provenance;
- claims such as “official,” “certified,” “Odoc Cloud,” “partner,” or “security approved.”

Forks are encouraged to choose a distinct primary name and visual identity, identify
their changes, link to corresponding source, and avoid official release coordinates.
An unmodified redistribution may make accurate provenance statements but should identify
who provides its support.

## Security and deceptive use

Do not use a confusing identity for credential collection, malicious packages, or
diversion from security updates. Security-relevant impersonation may be reported through
the operational private channel required by [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); platform abuse
may also be reported to the hosting or registry provider. Ordinary identity questions
need a public contact before the first release.

## Replacement-name decision before architecture bootstrap

Before `P0-002`, the project owner must select a replacement candidate and authorize
and complete an appropriate availability review across relevant software/project/package/
domain, company, common-law, and trademark sources. The review records its jurisdictions,
classes, sources, limitations, and who, if anyone, can grant rights in a future logo.
Candidate selection is not clearance or adoption. Only after that review and a separate
recorded owner decision may the project adopt the replacement for package namespaces,
artifact/image/chart names, UI branding, or a coordinated repository rename.

Repeat review before the first tagged release and 1.0, and when entering a materially
new jurisdiction or commercial use. This document provides no assurance that any name
is available anywhere and is not legal advice.

Material changes follow [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). A name-policy change cannot
retroactively remove copyright-license rights already granted in the software.
